Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy covers the website at dotfiles.marcopontili.com. The dotfiles themselves run only on your own machines and send nothing to this website.

Controller

Marco Pontili. Contact: marcopontili.com/contact.

Analytics: Matomo (self-hosted)

This website uses a self-hosted, cookieless installation of Matomo to measure traffic in aggregate.

  • No cookies or similar identifiers are stored on your device.
  • IP addresses are anonymised before they are stored.
  • The browser's Do Not Track signal is honoured: with it switched on, no visit is recorded.
  • Data processed: anonymised IP address, pages visited, referrer, browser and device type, and outbound link clicks.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in privacy-friendly, aggregate traffic measurement.
  • Place of processing: the controller's own server in the European Union (Italy). No data is shared with third parties.

Hosting

The website is hosted by Netsons S.r.l. (Italy), acting as processor. The host processes server log data, including IP addresses, as necessary to deliver the website and keep it operating and secure. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in operating and securing the website. Place of processing: European Union (Italy).

Cookies

This website sets no cookies and uses no local storage for tracking. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Retention

Personal data is kept only as long as the purpose requires. Analytics data is stored in anonymised, aggregate form. Server logs are kept for the host's standard rotation period.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and port your data, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. To exercise any of these rights, use the contact page above. You may lodge a complaint with the data protection authority where you live or work. The same rights and the same contact route apply wherever you are in the world.

Changes

This policy is updated when the website's processing changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
